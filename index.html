Data and AI operations for a sports-lottery retailer

Licensed Taiwan Sports Lottery retailer, Digital Operations Partner Lead · Jul. 2023 – present

A two-location, government-licensed retailer had member, betting and partner data in separate places, so nobody could see which members were drifting away or which partner channels actually brought turnover. I lead a 7–10 person team (sports analysts, content, events, engineering) running its digital operations.

I built a data pipeline and BI dashboard in Python, SQLite and Dash that unifies 3 data sources across both locations (19 months, 29K+ member-day records), with RFM segmentation, churn alerts and partner commission tracking, covered by 151 automated tests. Channel attribution showed partner and KOL channels drove about two-thirds of tagged member turnover; recruiting sports KOCs grew average monthly active members by 33% (113 to 150).

On top of the data, I designed a multi-agent operations system: 5 AI agents with permission limits, human kill switches and LLM-as-a-judge content review that produce a daily KPI brief for the team.

Evaluating an AI customer-service agent

Cathay Life Insurance, GenAI Intern · Jul. – Dec. 2025

Before a service agent goes live, the team needs to know whether it follows the required flow and treats customers well, not only whether it sounds fluent. I designed an evaluation on the Agenta platform that scores each conversation with four weighted checks: three rule-based and one LLM-as-a-judge.

The test set combines scripted multi-turn conversations with 30 negative cases that target staying within role, staying on topic, explaining clearly and acknowledging emotion. On a 60-case benchmark, the judge's pass/fail calls matched human labels in 87% of cases (52 of 60).

Retrieval for bank customer questions

E.SUN Bank AI RAG Competition (T-Brain), 2024 · team of 3 · shortlisted in the preliminary round

Given a customer question, the system must find the one correct document among insurance policies, financial reports and FAQs. I tested how accurately each candidate embedding model retrieved the right document, and built the reranking step for financial-report questions, where long reports are split into overlapping chunks and embedding scores are fused with BM25.

On the 150 official sample questions, a single reproducible pipeline reaches 88.67% precision@1, up from the official baseline of 71.33%.

Methods were compared on the same 150 public questions; there is no held-out set.

Prediction from text and behavior data

Big Data & Business Analytics, National Taiwan University, spring 2025 · two team projects, 6 people

Will an online order fail?

Using 3.4 million e-commerce orders from 91APP, where about 12% fail, the team asked whether shopping behavior before an order signals failure. I built the DNN, LSTM and GRU models on counts of 18 behaviors in the 72 hours before each order. The DNN reached AUC 0.85, and 0.86 on orders that had behavior records.

View project

Can news and forum posts predict a stock's direction?

For Innolux (3481), I did the preprocessing: filtering 9,139 forum and news posts by keyword, labeling them by the price move 5 days later, and building TF-IDF and chi-square features. Across six classifiers, the best ensemble (random forest, naive Bayes and XGBoost) reached 70% accuracy on a held-out test split. In a stricter day-by-day backtest over two years, it was right 60% of the time on the 3,407 calls it made confidently.
